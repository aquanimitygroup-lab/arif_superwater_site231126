About the study

A 12-week, single-centre, participant-blinded randomised controlled trial of Super Water with Theraeon HD in adults with type 2 diabetes and hypertension, conducted at CGHR, Diabetic Association of Bangladesh. The manuscript is under peer review at Health Science Reports, published by Wiley.

Bhowmik B. et al., Effect of Multi-Mineral Drinking Water on Postprandial Glycaemia in Adults with Type 2 Diabetes and Hypertension in Bangladesh: A Randomised Controlled Trial. Trial registration ISRCTN13257145 · Ethics approval BADAS-ERC/EC/25/62.

Super Water is an enhanced water. It is not a medicine and is not intended to diagnose, treat, cure or prevent any disease. If you have diabetes, high blood pressure or another medical condition, keep taking your prescribed treatment and talk to your doctor.
